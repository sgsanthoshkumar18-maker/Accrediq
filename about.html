AQcredix — Accreditation & Quality Implementation Guidance Platform

Most hospitals don't fail NABH.
They fail to understand it.

Teams read the standards and apply them — but without truly understanding what the words demand, gaps slip through. The assessor, who does understand, finds them. AQcredix closes that gap: real understanding, so the Non-Conformity never happens in the first place.

The mission

Make quality understandable for everyone in the hospital.

From the housekeeping trolley to the director's office, every person carries a piece of accreditation. AQcredix teaches each of them their part — clearly, memorably, and with the assessor's perspective built in.

We're an independent education and training forum, open to any healthcare professional working to make their organisation safer and better.

The roadmap

From a website to a movement.

AQcredix starts as a learning forum — and is built to grow into a community, a conference series, and one day a recognised certifying body.

Phase 1 · Now

The learning forum

Public, free, SEO-first content. NABH standards in depth, department roles, KPIs, quality tools, and assessor videos feeding a YouTube channel.

Phase 2 · Growth

Community & programs

Accounts and progress tracking, learning paths and certificates, webinars and offline workshops, and the NABL & iCOMPLY frameworks.

Phase 3 · Authority

Conferences & certification

A membership community, a mock-audit simulator, benchmarking, foreign accreditations, and AQcredix as a recognised certifying body.
