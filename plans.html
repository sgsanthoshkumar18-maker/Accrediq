Questions worth asking

Is it per user?

No. One subscription covers up to 15 accounts — enough for one per department in most hospitals. Each person signs in as themselves, which is what makes the record of who did what stand up to an assessor, and each is scoped to their own department. Your Quality Manager and Director see everything; everybody else sees only what they are given.

What happens to our data if we stop?

It stays yours. You can export everything to Excel and JSON at any time, without asking us, and the export button is in the product rather than behind a support request.

Does this replace a consultant?

No, and we would not claim it does. It replaces the spreadsheets, the reminders and the folder of forms that a consultant otherwise has to untangle each visit.

Is AQcredix an accrediting body?

No. We are not affiliated with NABH, QCI or any accreditation organisation, and using this platform does not confer or guarantee accreditation. The published standard remains the authority.
