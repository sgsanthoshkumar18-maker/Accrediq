Every department, whatever its size, also gets: its own risk matrix and risk register with owners and mitigation status; a timed internal audit it runs itself, exporting to a six-sheet Excel workbook; incident reporting with a one-hour window, four-level classification and RCA feeding into CAPA; and a renewal calendar covering calibration, preventive maintenance, AMC, licence renewal and inspection. Departments with no quality staff and no budget of their own — laundry, kitchen, housekeeping, mortuary — get exactly what the wards get. Nothing is reserved for a larger plan, because there is no larger plan.
