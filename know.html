Know Your Hospital.

Describe what your hospital or department currently does, in your own words. Pick a chapter, then the specific standard you are checking. AQcredix works through that standard element by element, tells you what each one is really asking for, gives a plain everyday example, and shows exactly what to add to close the gap.

Working one standard at a time gives genuinely specific feedback, element by element.

Write in full sentences, as if explaining to a colleague. The more you describe, the more accurate the comparison — a few lines will under-report your alignment.

What this is. A structured self-check that compares the topics you mention against what each Objective Element covers. It is a learning and preparation aid — not an assessment, not an audit result, and not a substitute for reading the official standard or for a real gap analysis by a qualified assessor.
