Refund & Cancellation Policy

How to cancel, when a refund is and is not given, and what happens to your records if a subscription ends.

Last updated: 21 August 2026

This policy forms part of our Terms of Service and uses the same wording as section 4 of that document.

1. Cancelling

You can cancel at any time from the workspace. No email, no phone call, no notice period. Cancelling stops the next payment. It does not refund the period you have already paid for, and you keep full access until that period ends.

Subscriptions run for the stated period and do not renew automatically unless that was stated at the point of purchase.

2. The free trial

New subscribers may start a 7-day free trial. Nothing is charged during it. You may cancel at any point in those seven days from the workspace and no payment will be taken.

If you do not cancel, the subscription begins at the end of the trial and the first payment is taken then. In line with the Reserve Bank of India's e-mandate framework, you will receive a notification at least 24 hours before any debit, stating the amount and date and offering the option to opt out of that payment. You will always be told before money is taken, and never charged without warning.

3. Refunds

Payments are not refundable once taken. The trial exists so that the decision is made before any money changes hands: seven days is enough to enter real data and judge whether the platform suits your hospital, and we would rather you cancel during the trial than pay and regret it.

This means a subscription cannot be refunded because your hospital later changed priorities, reorganised, decided not to pursue accreditation this cycle, or wanted features we do not offer. Those are reasons to cancel before the next renewal, not grounds for a refund of a period already paid for.

Where the fault is ours

That is different. If a defect in the platform prevents you from using what you paid for, tell us. We will investigate, and where the problem is genuinely on our side and cannot be resolved in reasonable time, we will discuss a refund or an extension of your term.

We say this because a blanket refusal that ignores our own failures would not be fair, and would not stand.

How to ask

Email support.aqcredix@gmail.com with the account email and what went wrong. We aim to reply within 3 working days. Where a refund is agreed, it is returned to the original payment method, normally within 5 to 7 working days of approval, and the timing after that is with your bank rather than with us.

4. If a subscription lapses

Your data is not deleted when a subscription ends. You keep the ability to sign in and export everything you have entered for at least 90 days after expiry, and we will tell you before anything is removed.

The export button is in the product rather than behind a support request, so getting your records out never depends on us replying.

5. Payment methods

Payment is by UPI and Razorpay. Where payment is made by UPI with a reference number, access is granted after we verify the payment manually, which may take up to 2 working days. If a payment is taken but access is not granted, that is a fault on our side and is refundable under section 3.
