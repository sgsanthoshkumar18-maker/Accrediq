5 Whys: a medication error

Watch a real problem drilled to its root — the difference between blaming a nurse and fixing a system.

  1. Why was the wrong dose given? The label was misread.
  2. Why was it misread? Two strengths look almost identical.
  3. Why are they stored together? No high-alert separation.
  4. Why no separation? The SOP doesn't require it.
  5. Why doesn't it? Root cause — fix the SOP, not the nurse.
