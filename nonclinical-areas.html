The areas behind the care.

Document review, quality management, HR, medical records, facilities, CSSD and the rest — the non-clinical areas an assessor examines, each with its element groups, quick list and indicators.

Source: NABH assessor checklist (5th Edition). Two chapters were renamed in the 6th Edition — HIC became IPC (Infection Prevention and Control) and CQI became PSQ (Patient Safety and Quality Improvement). Codes are shown as printed in the checklist and cross-checked against the 6th Edition element data. Where a code no longer resolves, this is stated plainly rather than guessed at.
