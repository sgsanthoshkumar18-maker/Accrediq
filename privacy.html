2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide directly

  • Mock Surveyor assessments — the name, email address and department you enter when starting an assessment, together with your answers and score.
  • Uploaded documents — SOPs you upload during an assessment or for review.

Important: at present the Mock Surveyor stores your name, email, results and history entirely within your own browser (localStorage). This data is not transmitted to us or to any server. Uploaded SOP files are read in your browser and are never uploaded anywhere. Clearing your browser data permanently erases this history, and anyone else using the same device and browser profile can see it.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Hosting logs — our host ([ Vercel ]) records standard server information such as IP address, browser type, referring page and time of request, used for security and reliability.
  • Preferences — your theme and colour-palette choices are stored in your browser only.

2.3 Third-party services your browser contacts

Some pages request data directly from third parties. When that happens your browser contacts them and they may see your IP address:

  • World Bank Indicators API — country health indicators.
  • WHO Global Health Observatory — health statistics, via a proxy on this site.
  • OpenStreetMap / Overpass API — hospital locations.
  • Public CDNs (jsDelivr, unpkg, Cloudflare, Google Fonts) — libraries and fonts.

Each operates under its own privacy policy, which we do not control.

3. Cookies

AQcredix does not set advertising or tracking cookies. We use browser localStorage for the preferences and assessment history described above. This is not shared across sites and is not used to profile you.

If this changes: if you later add analytics or advertising, update this section and add a cookie-consent mechanism first — particularly for visitors in the EU/UK.

4. How we use information

  • To operate the site and its learning tools.
  • To remember your preferences and your own assessment history on your device.
  • To maintain security and diagnose faults.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with advertisers.

5. Data retention

Assessment history and preferences remain in your browser until you clear browser data. Hosting logs are retained by our host under their own schedule.
