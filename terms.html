2. What AQcredix is — and what it is not

AQcredix is a software tool that helps a hospital organise its NABH accreditation work: browsing standards, scheduling committee meetings and recurring obligations, maintaining an equipment and licence register, recording rounds and audits, and tracking findings to closure.

AQcredix is not an accrediting body. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting on behalf of NABH, QCI, JCI, ISQua or any accreditation or certification organisation. Using this platform does not confer accreditation, does not guarantee accreditation, and does not guarantee any assessment outcome.

AQcredix does not provide clinical, legal or regulatory advice. Explanatory content, assessor guidance and gap descriptions represent our professional interpretation. The published NABH standard remains the sole authority. Clinical decisions remain the responsibility of the treating clinician, and compliance decisions remain the responsibility of the hospital.
